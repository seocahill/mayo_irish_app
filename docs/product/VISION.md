## What this is for

Today "Mayo Irish App" (working name) is a platform for learning how to understand and speak the Mayo / North Connacht dialects of Irish. It compliments "Abairt" by consumign it's content and adding hetrogenuous sources (irishlanguagemayo, Mach and Fhailigh et al), based on the skeleton of a proven learning apprach (Ô Siadhail) and cutting edge tech (abair.ie ASR, inference, TTS) to deliver an holistic learning experience

## Who it serves, and what great looks like for them

- **Course Admins**: native speakers, volunteers and editors who design edit, update and hone the course.
- **Learners**: people who come to learn the dialect to the level they choose.
- **Other software**: the course app, researchers and the models below, all
  through a first-class API and MCP server.

I'm happy to let the best-in-class peers define "great" rather than spell it
out here. The architect should name the peers it compares against in each
proposal and say where we fall short. In practice:

- **The Learning Interface** follows MOOC, SPR etc best practice
- **Listening and watching** meets the standard of mainstream audio and video
  streaming apps on every screen, phone first.
- **Transcribing** meets the standard of the best transcription and subtitle
  editors.
- **Reference data** (places, speaker names, map coordinates) is accurate,
  up to date and a pleasure to browse.
- **The API and MCP** are products in their own right: complete, documented
  and stable.


## Where it's going

The key insight is that language learning is a long frustating process. 

The goal is to reach the point where have a reasonably measurable system where we can project in a useful way to learners "how far along" they are in terms of the requried proficiencies. E.g. vocab, speech practice, listening comprehension, etc.

## What it should never become

A general learning platform. There is one singular goal. More speakers of the Mayo dialects.
